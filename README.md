# AI Company Adoption Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze how companies are adopting artificial intelligence, their level of AI investment, and their approach to AI risk management.

The project transforms raw company-level data into actionable insights that can help identify adoption patterns, investment levels, and areas of AI-related risk.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze AI adoption stages across companies.
* Measure the percentage of companies with an AI budget.
* Examine AI adoption across countries and continents.
* Identify companies in early stages of AI adoption.
* Evaluate AI risk management scores.
* Create an interactive dashboard for exploring AI adoption patterns.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI** — Data visualization and dashboard development
* **DAX** — Measures and KPI calculations
* **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
* **Excel/CSV** — Data source and preparation

## 🔍 Dashboard Features

* AI adoption stage analysis
* AI budget analysis
* AI risk management analysis
* Country and continent filtering
* Global, Africa, and Nigeria views
* Interactive KPI cards and visualizations
* DAX-powered metrics

## 💡 Key Questions Explored

1. How widely are companies adopting AI?
2. What proportion of companies have allocated a budget for AI?
3. How many companies are still in the early stages of AI adoption?
4. How does AI adoption vary across regions?
5. What does the data indicate about companies' AI risk management practices?
